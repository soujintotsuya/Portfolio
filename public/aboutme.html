Who is "Soujin"?

That's Me! My name's Leon, tho I prefer to go by my alias "Soujin" in a professional context. I am at the end of my twenties and always had a great interest in anything tech, tho I never really got into it because I did not think I'd have the qualification for it, well here I am.

div
id="Background"

My journey began after a long period of mental struggles and not really knowing what to do with myself. Thankfully in Germany there is social security measures in place, which lead me to participate in a job orientation program at the "BFW Stralsund" during which I also learned that I was anything but underqualified to persue my interest in tech and so I made the decision to enlist in an apprenticeship as a Software Developer (Fachinformatiker für Anwendungsentwicklung), which, as of right now, yet still lies ahead of me.
